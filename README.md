# Python_Sem 6 Project
Project Title
PoshanSathi – Smart Nutrition & Welfare Tracking System for Anganwadi Centres

Tagline: "Digitally empowering Anganwadi Centres for healthier mothers and children."

Problem Statement

Anganwadi Centres play a crucial role in monitoring the health and nutrition of:

 Children (0–6 years)
 Pregnant women
 Lactating mothers

However, many centres still maintain records manually, which leads to:

Difficulty tracking child growth over time.
Missed vaccination schedules.
Delayed identification of malnourished children.
Lack of centralized reports for supervisors.
Poor awareness of welfare schemes among beneficiaries.

The goal of PoshanSathi is to digitize these records, simplify daily work for Anganwadi workers, and provide useful insights to supervisors.
